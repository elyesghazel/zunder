What zunder sends over the network

zunder runs on your computer. It sends nothing anywhere unless one of these applies:

- Update check: once a day zunder asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest release and
  shows a note on the start screen. Turn it off in Settings > General > Check for updates.
- Plugins: Settings > Plugins and "zunder plugin" download the plugin list and plugins
  from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), only when you open that page or run the command.
- Currency conversion: typing a conversion like "20 usd to chf" loads exchange rates from
  open.er-api.com, at most every 12 hours.
- Login and sync: only after you log in (Settings > Account or "zunder login"), zunder
  syncs your settings and favorites to a private gist on your own GitHub account.
  Plugin tokens are never synced.
- Web search and links open in your browser, which then talks to that site.

zunder has no analytics, no telemetry and no account of its own.
Plugins you install are separate programs and can do their own networking.

Code signing policy: https://github.com/elyesghazel/zunder/blob/main/docs/code-signing.md
